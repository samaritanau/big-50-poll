Where we stay

Taipei for two nights, Jiaoxi for three

Taipei on Monday and Tuesday nights. Jiaoxi on Wednesday, Thursday and Friday nights.

Nights 1 and 2 · Mon 28 and Tue 29

Taipei

First choice: City Suites Taipei Nanxi. Close to Ningxia Night Market and Dihua Street, with twin and family room options.

Other option: citizenM Taipei North Gate. Large shared space and a 24-hour bar, but small rooms with one king bed and no twins.

Nights 3, 4 and 5 · Wed 30 to Fri 2

Jiaoxi

First choice: Yamagata Kaku. Central, lower priced and with a private hot-spring bath in every room.

Other option: MU Jiaoxi. Larger resort with twin rooms, pools and a bar.
