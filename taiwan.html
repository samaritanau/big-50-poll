Where we stay

Two hotels across five nights

We stay in one Taipei hotel for the first two nights, then one Jiaoxi hotel for the last three. No changing hotels every morning.

Nights 1 and 2 · Mon 28 and Tue 29

Taipei

First choice: City Suites Taipei Nanxi. It is close to Ningxia Night Market and Dihua Street, with more room for twins and families.

Other option: citizenM Taipei North Gate. Better shared spaces and a 24-hour bar, but the rooms are small and only have one king bed.

Nights 3, 4 and 5 · Wed 30 to Fri 2

Jiaoxi

First choice: Yamagata Kaku. Central, good value and a private hot-spring bath in every room.

Other option: MU Jiaoxi. A larger resort with roomy twins, pools, a bar and more places for the group to hang out.
