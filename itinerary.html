  4. Thu1Oct
    Yilan · Night 4 Confirmed

    Caoling Historic Trail

    Leave the rental car in the hotel car park. After breakfast, take the roughly 09:36 train from Jiaoxi to Dali, arriving around 10am. The trail begins just beyond Dali Visitor Centre, through the Qingyun Temple grounds. Start walking around 10:15.

    The full walk through to Fulong is about 8.5 to 9 km with roughly 320 m of climbing. The Dali side climbs straight away on stone steps. Allow about four hours including lunch and stops: reach Yakou Pass late in the morning, descend through Yuanwangkeng Riverside Park and continue to Fulong. Expect to arrive around 2:30pm to 3pm.

    Bring lunch and at least 1.5 litres of water each. Finish with a bento, coffee or beer in Fulong, then take the next suitable train to Jiaoxi. Recheck the TRA timetable closer to departure. The silver grass may be starting to flower, but its main season is in November.

    Dinner to choose

    Wushi Port dinner

    Crab Boss Live Seafood: the leading option for a relaxed harbour dinner. Live seafood, sashimi, grilled fish, squid, noodles, vegetables and beer. Most cooked dishes are around NT$280 to NT$380, roughly AUD 12 to 17, with live seafood priced separately.

    Happiness No. 36: a simpler local seafood restaurant directly in the harbour area, with shared dishes, beer and straightforward pricing.

    Wu's Harbour Cuisine: the smarter option, serving a seafood-led set menu with sea views at Wai'ao, just north of the port. Reservations are important.

    OA Pizza: the easy fallback at the port, with pizza, pasta and local draft beer, including Jim & Dad's.
