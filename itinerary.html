    Supplier to call

    Bike options

    Best lead: Xiefa Bicycle, beside Toucheng Station. It opens from 9am and advertises bicycles and electric vehicles from NT$600. Confirm that the electric vehicles are pedal-assist bikes, that four are available, and that the van can collect them. Phone: 03-931-1089.

    Backup: Yongzhen Seaside Bicycle Rental. It is directly on the Zhuangwei coast. The listed bikes appear to be regular bicycles, but the route is flat. Its Friday opening time is listed as 11:30am, so using it would push the ride towards lunchtime.

    Xiefa map ↗ Xiefa details ↗ Yongzhen map ↗ Yongzhen details ↗
    Dinner to choose

    Wushi Port dinner

    Crab Boss Live Seafood: the leading option for a relaxed harbour dinner. Live seafood, sashimi, grilled fish, squid, noodles, vegetables and beer. Most cooked dishes are around NT$280 to NT$380, with live seafood priced separately.

    Happiness No. 36: a simpler local seafood restaurant directly in the harbour area, with shared dishes, beer and straightforward pricing.

    Wu's Harbour Cuisine: the smarter option, serving a seafood-led set menu with sea views at Wai'ao, just north of the port. Reservations are important.

    OA Pizza: the easy fallback at the port, with pizza, pasta and local draft beer, including Jim & Dad's.

  6. Sat3Oct
    Jiaoxi to Taoyuan Airport Confirmed

    Fly out in the afternoon

    Private van: travel directly from Yamagata Kaku to the terminal. The drive is usually around 1 hour 30 minutes, but allow up to two hours for traffic. Plan to leave Jiaoxi about five hours before the flight.

    Train: walk to Jiaoxi Station, take a reserved TRA express to Taipei Main Station, then walk through the station to A1 and take the Airport MRT Express. Allow 1 hour 10 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes on the TRA train, 15 to 25 minutes for the transfer, and 35 minutes to Terminal 1 or about 39 minutes to Terminal 2. With waiting time, allow 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 45 minutes from the hotel to the terminal.

    Cost comparison: the express-train route is about NT$441 per person, roughly AUD 20, or AUD 80 for four. The private van is roughly AUD 135 to 150 for the vehicle. The saving is modest once split four ways, and the train means handling suitcases through Taipei Main Station. Exact TRA times for 3 October should be checked when bookings open four weeks ahead.
